FAQ

What age is Happy Octo Puzzle for?

Designed for children aged 3 to 8. Large puzzle pieces and simple drag-and-drop make it accessible even for toddlers.

Is it free?

The current release is free to download and play, with no subscriptions. See Google Play for current availability and pricing.

Are there any ads?

No ads at all. Kids can play without any interruptions or distractions.

Does it work without internet?

Puzzle play works offline. Downloading the app and using store services require a connection.
