Our approach

Rules we don't bend.

They apply to every app we ship, across every brand, with no exceptions for a good quarter.

No ads, ever

We sell nothing about the people using our apps — not their attention, not screen space, not data. Least of all in an app made for a five-year-old.

Private by default

Our apps run offline and keep their data on the device unless someone deliberately decides otherwise. No account to get started. No analytics profiles.

No dark patterns

No streaks, countdowns, or manufactured urgency. If someone puts the phone down and doesn't come back today, the app did its job.

Focused and finished

Each app does one thing properly, then gets out of the way. We ship narrow and complete rather than broad and half-built.

Honest pricing

Terms stated upfront. Nothing removed from a working app to sell it back later, and no subscription prompt at the worst possible moment.

Built by hand

Every screen is designed, coded, and tested by the people whose name is on it. Independent studio, deliberate releases, one standard.

The studio

Apps, and mountains.

Appmonto is exactly what it sounds like: app and monte — the mountain. The studio works out of St. Johann im Pongau, in the Salzburg Alps, where the view out of the window is a decent daily argument for building things that hold up: direct, durable, and without unnecessary decoration.

We are an independent studio and answer to nobody's roadmap. Every app is designed, built, and published here — from the first sketch to the store listing. That keeps the range narrow, and it keeps the standards ours.

Our apps are published on Google Play under the studio's brands. Each brand keeps its own site, its own support address, and its own privacy policy, linked above.
