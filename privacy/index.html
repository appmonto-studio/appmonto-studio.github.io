Privacy

Privacy notice

This page covers the website appmonto.com only. Each application has its own privacy policy, published on the site of its brand.

What this website collects

Nothing. This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, embeds no analytics, no advertising, no social media widgets, and no external fonts or scripts. There is no login, no form, and no comment function.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Like any web server, GitHub's infrastructure processes technical connection data — including your IP address, the requested address, and the time of the request — in order to deliver the page and to keep the service secure. We have no access to these server logs and receive no reports from them.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating a secure, functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

Email contact

If you write to hello@appmonto.com, your message and address are processed only to answer you, and kept only as long as needed for that (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Nothing from an email is used for marketing or passed to anyone else.

Links to other sites

Our brand sites (kidlogicloft.com, getelia.app) and the Google Play Store are separate destinations with their own privacy terms. Following a link takes you outside this site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability regarding personal data we hold — in practice, that means the contents of any email correspondence. Write to hello@appmonto.com.

You may also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna) — dsb.gv.at.
